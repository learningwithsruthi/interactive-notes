HowHow to take this test

50 questions, every topic, mixed together. The questions are new: none of them repeat the topic practice. The order shuffles each time you load the page.

Time
The real AP exam gives you 80 minutes for 55 questions, about 87 seconds each. Aim for about 70 minutes here.
Grading
Nothing is graded until you click Submit test at the bottom. Then every question shows the answer and explanation, and you get a score for each topic.
Guessing
There is no penalty for wrong answers on the AP exam. Never leave one blank.
After
Any topic under 80% gets a "review" tag. Go back to that topic's page and redo its practice.
