1Algebra: Quadratic Functions

A quadratic function has the form $f(x) = ax^2 + bx + c$. Its graph is a parabola. The roots (zeros) are where the parabola crosses the x-axis.

$f(x) = x^2 - 5x + 6 = (x-2)(x-3)$

Roots: $x = 2$ and $x = 3$. The vertex is at $x = -b/(2a) = 2.5$, $y = -0.25$.

3Geometry: 2D Figures

For a right triangle with legs $a$ and $b$ and hypotenuse $c$, the Pythagorean theorem says $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$. With $a=3$ and $b=4$, the hypotenuse is $c=5$ — the famous 3-4-5 triangle.

Right Triangle (3-4-5)
$a^2 + b^2 = c^2 \;\Rightarrow\; 3^2 + 4^2 = 9 + 16 = 25 = 5^2$ ✓

Below, a regular hexagon with side length $s$. Area: $A = \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}s^2$, perimeter: $P = 6s$.

6Calculus: Definite Integrals

The definite integral $\int_a^b f(x)\,dx$ is the signed area between $f(x)$ and the x-axis from $a$ to $b$. For $f(x) = x^2$ from 0 to 2:

$\displaystyle \int_0^2 x^2 \, dx = \left[ \frac{x^3}{3} \right]_0^2 = \frac{8}{3} \approx 2.667$
Shaded Area Under f(x) = x² on [0, 2] (function-plot)

The derivative $f'(x)$ gives the slope of the tangent line at any point. For $f(x) = x^2$, $f'(x) = 2x$. At $x = 1.5$, the tangent has slope $3$.

Tangent Line at x = 1.5 (function-plot, hover to see live derivative)
7Vectors: 2D and 3D

A vector has both magnitude and direction. To add two vectors, place the tail of the second vector at the head of the first; the resultant goes from the original tail to the final head.

2D Vector Addition: $\vec{a} + \vec{b}$ (head-to-tail)
$\vec{a} = \langle 4, 2 \rangle, \;\vec{b} = \langle 3, 3 \rangle, \;\vec{a}+\vec{b} = \langle 7, 5 \rangle$

8Matrices: 2×2 Operations

A matrix is a rectangular array of numbers. Matrix multiplication is row-by-column: $(AB)_{ij} = \sum_k A_{ik}B_{kj}$.
